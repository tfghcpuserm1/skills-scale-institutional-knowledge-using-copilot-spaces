# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads or Engineering Managers translate strategic priorities into executable engineering plans, maintain team capacity, and help the team deliver work predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Guide delivery planning, sprint sequencing, and resourcing
- Manage engineering trade-offs and risk escalation
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies and milestone health
- Support Developers in execution quality, planning, and delivery readiness
- Partner with Product Managers on scope decisions and technical feasibility

### Goals
- Deliver predictable team output and healthy engineering flow
- Improve delivery quality and reduce avoidable bottlenecks
- Align engineering work with product priorities and stakeholder commitments

### Typical Communication
- Delivery planning and sprint reviews
- Engineering risk and dependency updates
- Escalation discussions with Product and Project leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on scheduling, dependencies, and risk tracking
- Partners with Product Managers on priority trade-offs and scope decisions
- Supports Developers with planning clarity, technical throughput, and quality expectations

---

## UX/UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers ensure the team builds usable, customer-centered solutions and validates decisions with real user input.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights
- Define user flows, wireframes, and interface requirements
- Maintain usability and accessibility considerations throughout delivery
- Validate product decisions against user needs and business outcomes
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams on design feasibility

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and adoption
- Turn customer insight into actionable product decisions
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated experience assumptions

### Typical Communication
- Discovery and design review sessions
- User feedback and usability findings
- Design rationale and acceptance criteria validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate customer problems into experience requirements
- Consults with Developers on feasibility, implementation constraints, and interaction details
- Coordinates with Project Managers on design milestones, handoffs, and dependency timing
- Supports QA/Testing by clarifying usability and acceptance expectations

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects set technical direction, protect system integrity, and help the team navigate design complexity and integration risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide system design and architecture choices
- Record architecture decisions and trade-offs
- Identify technical dependencies, integration points, and non-functional requirements
- Support technical risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Mentor Developers and review implementation quality

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable and scalable technical foundation
- Reduce avoidable rework and integration failures
- Align technical decisions with product goals and delivery constraints

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency and integration planning
- Technical risk and escalation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Delivery Leads and Developers to guide implementation decisions
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers when technical constraints affect scope, sequencing, or milestones
- Helps QA/Testing understand critical risk areas and edge cases

---

## Release/DevOps or Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Release/DevOps and SRE roles ensure the team can move software safely, monitor it in production, and recover quickly from issues.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment workflows, environments, and operational readiness
- Define monitoring, alerting, and observability requirements
- Prepare rollback plans and coordinate release safety checks
- Support production health and incident readiness
- Partner with teams on deployment communication and operational impacts

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and improve deployment confidence
- Increase operational resilience and system observability
- Support smooth handoff from delivery to support and production operations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Post-deployment verification and incident updates
- Operational readiness and rollback coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and QA/Testing on release quality and readiness
- Works with Project Managers on deployment timing and stakeholder communication
- Supports Product Managers and support teams with release impact awareness and known issues

---

## Security and Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners protect the team from avoidable compliance, data, or security risks, and help teams build secure-by-default practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review threats, privacy risks, and compliance requirements
- Guide secure design, testing, and remediation efforts
- Support security review gates and incident response planning
- Help define risk ownership and mitigation actions
- Coordinate targeted follow-up with technical teams and stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy exposure
- Ensure products meet required standards and controls
- Build trust with customers, stakeholders, and regulators

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and privacy discussions
- Incident triage and escalation updates
- Risk and remediation tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Engages early with Product Managers and Technical Leads on security and compliance constraints
- Works with Developers to remediate findings and reduce residual risk
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk communication and release gate requirements

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring real-world customer impact and service readiness into planning and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer pain points, support trends, and operational constraints
- Surface runbook needs, known issues, and service readiness requirements
- Help define launch and support communication expectations
- Validate that product changes are operationally practical and supportable

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and support readiness
- Reduce avoidable post-release friction and escalations
- Align delivery with operational realities and service quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- Support intake and issue reviews
- Operational readiness and launch planning discussions
- Post-release feedback and service impact reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on customer value and service quality outcomes
- Works with Project Managers on communication planning and coordination
- Shares customer-impact insights with Developers to inform implementation and triage priorities
- Supports QA/Testing and Release/DevOps on support readiness, known issues, and validation

## Data/Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics partners define how the team measures success, learns from product usage, and validates outcomes with evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define product metrics, instrumentation, and dashboard needs
- Support analysis of user behavior and business outcomes after release
- Help teams interpret signals from experimentation and usage data
- Connect product decisions to measurable impact and learning loops

### Goals
- Improve decision quality with evidence
- Measure customer value and operational outcomes
- Turn ambiguous goals into trackable signals

### Typical Communication
- KPI and metric definition discussions
- Reporting reviews and milestone updates
- Analysis findings and recommendation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers on outcome measurement and success criteria
- Partners with Developers on telemetry and dashboard requirements
- Provides reporting support to Project Managers for milestone and release reviews

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, resource alignment, and escalation support when project decisions or risks exceed team-level authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priority and business context
- Support funding, staffing, and cross-functional alignment
- Resolve escalated trade-offs and business-impacting decisions
- Maintain visibility to major outcomes, risks, and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure the project stays aligned to business value and strategic direction
- Support timely decisions that unblock delivery or reduce key risks
- Maintain accountability for business-level outcomes and sponsorship

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive updates
- Escalation decisions and strategic prioritization discussions
- Sponsor-level risk and dependency communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on business outcomes and prioritization
- Receives concise updates from Project Managers on health, milestones, and escalations
- Reviews delivery signals from Developers (typically through engineering leadership) when risk or execution concerns require sponsor support
- Provides decision authority when cross-functional trade-offs or risk issues require executive intervention

## Role flexibility and accountability

These personas represent adaptable responsibilities rather than mandatory separate headcount. On smaller teams, one person may cover multiple roles. The key is not whether every role exists as a distinct title, but whether the responsibilities are assigned, visible, and understood.

A practical rule for OctoAcme is:
- Product Manager owns product outcome and priority
- Project Manager owns plan, coordination, risk communication, and cross-team alignment
- Developers own implementation quality and delivery execution
- Additional roles provide consultation, decision support, release readiness, and domain expertise as needed

## Decision ownership, handoffs, and escalation

Clear accountability helps the team move quickly without confusion:
- Product Managers decide what is valuable and should be prioritized
- Project Managers coordinate delivery, dependencies, and communication across workstreams
- Developers decide implementation details within agreed scope and constraints
- Technical Leads/Architects and Delivery Leads advise on technical feasibility and execution quality
- Security, Support, Data, and UX roles provide consultation and required sign-off for domain-specific concerns
- Executive Sponsors intervene when strategic decisions, resource issues, or business-impacting escalations require authority beyond the project team

Typical handoffs include:
- Problem definition: Product Manager + stakeholder input
- Planning: Product Manager + Project Manager + delivery and specialist roles
- Execution: Developers + Technical Lead + Delivery Lead
- Validation: QA/Testing + Support + Security + Data/Analytics as relevant
- Release: Release/DevOps + Project Manager + Product Manager + support stakeholders
- Retrospective: entire project group, with follow-up tracked by the Project Manager

## Process participation map

| Stage | Core participants | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initiation | Product Manager, Project Manager, Executive Sponsor, UX/Research, Security/Privacy, Data/Analytics | Validate problem, stakeholders, success metrics, scope, and constraints |
| Planning | Product Manager, Project Manager, Delivery Lead, Technical Lead, Developers, UX/Research, QA/Testing, Security/Privacy, Data/Analytics | Define backlog, estimates, milestones, risk, and readiness requirements |
| Execution | Developers, Delivery Lead, Technical Lead, UX/Research, QA/Testing, Security/Privacy, Data/Analytics | Build, review, test, and manage technical and operational risks |
| Release | Release/DevOps, Developers, QA/Testing, Product Manager, Project Manager, Support/Operations, Security/Privacy | Verify readiness, communicate impact, and monitor rollout |
| Retrospective | Project Manager, Product Manager, delivery team, Support/Operations, Data/Analytics, stakeholder partners | Capture learnings, process improvements, and next steps |
